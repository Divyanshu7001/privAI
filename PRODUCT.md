# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Everyday internet users and professionals posting on social media (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram) who need to protect their sensitive personal information (PII) and corporate data from accidental public exposure. They need a non-intrusive assistant that runs quietly in the background and only intervenes when a privacy risk is detected.

## Product Purpose
A client-side privacy assistant consisting of a browser extension and dashboard. It intercepts post publications, transcribes video audio, and scans text locally to flag sensitive data before publishing, helping users track and reduce their cumulative exposure risk.

## Positioning
Preventing privacy leaks in real-time, client-side, before you publish, while providing actionable analytics to analyze and reduce your cumulative digital footprint and risk exposure.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality**: Secure, minimalist, utility-first (reminiscent of developer tools like Vercel and Tailwind).
- **Tone**: Professional, precise, reassuring, and low-friction.
- **Emotional Goals**: Instill confidence, peace of mind, and control over private data.

## Anti-references
- Overly flashy, bright, or distracting gradient designs that feel cheap or game-like.
- Intrusive and anxiety-inducing modal popups or generic scary alerts.
- Cluttered settings pages and dashboard layouts that obscure critical privacy data.

## Design Principles
- **Friction with Purpose**: Introduce visual or interactive friction (e.g., alert dialogs) only when high-risk PII is about to be published; otherwise, stay completely out of the user's way.
- **Utility Over Ornament**: Prioritize clear data layouts, strong typography hierarchy, and precise alignment over decorative patterns.
- **Transparent Local Processing**: Design cues should clearly communicate that analysis happens client-side and that data remains private.
- **Actionable Intervention**: Never show a warning without a clear, immediate action to fix, ignore, or edit the flagged text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast & Hierarchy**: Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines with high-contrast text ratios for readability.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect system motion preferences by defaulting to instant or basic crossfade transitions for alerts and cards.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible keyboard shortcuts and focus states for the extension popup and dashboard controls.
